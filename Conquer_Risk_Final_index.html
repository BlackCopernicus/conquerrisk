Research: when models should be trusted.

The research program asks a prior question to model performance: does the available evidence contain the information required for the claim? It spans intervention-aware AI, information ceilings, transportability, scientific reproduction, biological trajectory analysis, and systemic AI risk. The full medical-AI portfolio is maintained at Conquer Medical Health.
